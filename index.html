FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before hiring.

Do you handle patios and retaining walls?

Yes. Patios, walkways, seating walls and retaining walls are core services.

Do you install drainage?

Yes. Drainage corrections can include French drains, catch basins, dry wells, downspout connections and grading.

What areas do you service?

Ryan's Landscape Construction serves Grafton and surrounding Central Massachusetts towns including Westborough, Shrewsbury, Millbury, Upton, Northbridge, Sutton, Hopkinton and nearby areas.

Can you restore the lawn after construction?

Yes. Loam, seed, hydroseed, sod and final grading can be included depending on the scope.
